## Problem & user

RocketShip PMs need evidence-based prioritization. Juno turns noisy Slack, tickets, and strategy docs into a ranked, cited backlog they can defend.

## Solution overview

**Retrieval strategy:** Modular RAG

How: Juno needs to ground every ranked claim in a citable source (system prompt), which requires an explicit retrieve-then-generate pipeline, not model memory. Where: the corpus spans four separate systems (Slack, Notion, Jira, transcripts) that don't share a schema, so indexing into one queryable store is required regardless of size. Modular RAG is the only pattern of the three that fits all three constraints at once.

## Retrieval requirements (RAG)

- **Sources:** Sources: Slack messages in #escalations and #support-triage tagged P0 or P1 only (not all Slack); Notion pages in the "RocketShip Product" workspace; Jira tickets in the ROCKET project, scoped to open tickets plus closed-in-last-30-days for recency comparison; call transcripts explicitly shared in-thread. Quantity: rolling 30-day window, not the full historical backlog.
- **Chunking / indexing:** Hybrid (Semantic + Keyword)
- **Grounding rule:** Every row of the ranked risk table must carry a citation — Slack thread link, Jira key, or transcript timestamp — with no exceptions. No row ships without one.
- **Freshness:** Event-triggered reindex on any new message/comment in a P0/P1-tagged thread or ticket, with an hourly batch refresh as backstop for everything else.

## Requirements

| 1 | Retrieval quality and latency | Must | Top-K = 15 candidates retrieved per query, reranked down to the 5 rows the system prompt's output table requires. Maintain p95 ≤ 4s. |

If ranking confidence is below 70%, Juno does not present a ranked finding at all; it hands off to the human PM. |

## Out of scope

Decisions that cannot be cited to a source in the knowledge base. Anything Juno is not allowed to retrieve or act on without a human.
