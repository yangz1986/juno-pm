## What "good" means

≥80% of rows receive no demote/correction within 24h; regenerate rate ≤15%; review-queue abandon rate (no PM action within 4h) ≤10%

Active: thumbs up/down on each ranked row; the "Not P0 / demote" and "Needs human review" buttons from the M4 Feedback Loop kill switch; free-text feedback on any thumbs-down. Passive: review-queue abandon rate (a draft sits with no PM action), regenerate-row clicks

## Golden set

150 anonymized P0/P1 threads with PM-curated expected top-5 rankings and correct Source IDs, versioned in 06-evals/golden-set/, refreshed quarterly and after any RAG architecture change (per the M3 PRD) or major incident

## Release gate

**Hard gates (auto-block):**

0% PII leakage. 0 critical safety fails on the human-eval layer. Citation-check fail → block. Any scope-boundary violation (e.g., a call to Salesforce or any non-approved source) → block, no exceptions.

**Soft gates (PM sign-off):**

P95 latency >4s requires PM justification before shipping (ties to the M3 PRD's p95≤4s target — crossing it doesn't block by itself, but it can't ship silently). Regenerate rate >15% or review-queue abandon rate >10% for 2 consecutive weeks requires PM review of the underlying cause.

**User-feedback layer (online):** cadence Per-request (real-time click logging) + weekly aggregate review; owner PM reviews the weekly aggregate; on-call PM triages immediately if ≥2 demotes land on the same recurring theme within 24h.
